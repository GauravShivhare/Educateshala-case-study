I am building Educatshala, a school management SaaS with role-based dashboards for Super Admin, School Admin, Principal, Office Staff, Teacher, Student, and Parent. I also want to use Codex to improve code quality, fix bugs, prepare a demo-ready MVP, and document the product properly.
