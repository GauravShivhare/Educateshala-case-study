## What are you building?

I am building EducateShala, a school management SaaS with role-based dashboards for Super Admin, School Admin, Principal, Office Staff, Teacher, Student, and Parent. It includes school management, user management, class-section workflows, email OTP forgot password, and AI school admin assistant workflows.

## What will you build during the hackathon?

During the hackathon, I built an AI School Admin Assistant inside EducateShala. It helps schools generate notices, summarize student records, assist staff with admin tasks, and answer common school workflow questions. I also used Codex to improve code quality, fix bugs, prepare a demo-ready MVP, and document the product properly.
